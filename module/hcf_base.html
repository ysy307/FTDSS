module subroutine calc_impedance_ratio_hcf(self, state, ratio)

Impedance factor of Hansson et al. (2004): , the ice fraction of the total (minus residual) water content. The impedance with was calibrated against the Mizoguchi columns using THIS definition; passing the raw volumetric ice content instead changes the meaning (and effective strength) of the calibrated factor.
