module subroutine apply_phase_change_temperature_correction_ftcms(self, T_old, T_new)

Correct temperature for nodes crossing T_melt during a Picard step. Uses the available-energy (Flerchinger-type) method: finds T_r satisfying via a Secant iteration, ensuring energy conservation at the phase-change front.
